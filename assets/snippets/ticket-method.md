Each ticket gives you a network with **one** fault, plus a symptom report. Load it without reading the ticket files; reading them spoils the exercise. Then work the problem methodically:

1. **Reproduce:** confirm the reported symptom yourself.
2. **Isolate:** narrow it down to the device, the layer and the feature. Record the commands you used and what each one ruled in or out.
3. **Root cause:** state precisely what is wrong and why it produces this symptom.
4. **Fix and verify:** correct it, and prove that the original symptom is gone *and* nothing else broke.
