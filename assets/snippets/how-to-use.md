| Label | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Concept** | A short refresher of the theory the step relies on. |
| **Predict** | Write down what you expect *before* you touch the device. Being wrong here is useful. |
| **Task** | What to achieve, with every value you need. Working out the commands is your job. |
| **Checkpoint** | How to prove the task worked. Do not move on until it passes. |
| **Question** | Explain what you observed, in writing. |
| **Break it** | Cause a failure on purpose, watch the symptoms, then repair it. |
| **Hint Hx.y** | Stuck for more than 10 minutes? Open Appendix A and read **one level at a time**. |

Scaffolding fades as you go. Early parts tell you which commands verify your work. Later parts only tell you what must be true, and you choose how to prove it.

The answer key lives in `solutions/`. Open it after you have a working result, or after you have used every hint level for a step.
